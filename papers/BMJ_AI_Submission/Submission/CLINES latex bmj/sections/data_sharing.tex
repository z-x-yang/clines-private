Source clinical notes were accessed under data-use agreements and are not publicly shareable. \new{The MIMIC-III dataset is available from PhysioNet\footnote{\url{https://physionet.org/content/mimiciii/}} under the PhysioNet credentialed-access process, which requires completion of CITI \emph{Human Subjects Research} training and signing of the dataset's Data Use Agreement. The CORAL dataset is available from PhysioNet\footnote{\url{https://physionet.org/content/curated-oncology-reports/1.0/}} under the same credentialed-access framework, requiring completion of CITI \emph{Data or Specimens Only Research} training and signing of the dataset's Data Use Agreement, as specified by the dataset custodians. The 4CE clinical-notes subset used in this study is the de-identified ``clinical notes, focusing on cases of patients with obesity and a history of COVID'' collection hosted on the Harvard Medical School DBMI Data Portal;\footnote{\url{https://portal.dbmi.hms.harvard.edu/projects/4ce-data-set/}} access is granted by the dataset custodians upon approval of the project-specific Data Use Agreement. General information about the 4CE consortium (Consortium for Clinical Characterization of COVID-19 by EHR) is available online.\footnote{\url{https://covidclinical.net/}} Cross-annotation files used for the inter-annotator agreement analysis (de-identified mention selections on six notes) are available from the corresponding author on reasonable request, subject to verification of the requestor's credentials for the source dataset. No additional patient-level data were generated by this study. Study-derived prompt templates and a public inference reference implementation of CLINES (single-note inference with user-supplied Azure OpenAI credentials) are released under the MIT License on GitHub.\footnote{\url{https://github.com/celehs/clines}}} An interactive, read-only i2b2 web demonstrator populated with CORAL results is also available online.\footnote{\url{celehs.hms.harvard.edu/i2b2_demo}}
