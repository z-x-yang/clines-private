\section{\new{Inter-Annotator Agreement: Per-Note Detail}}

\new{This section provides the per-note inter-annotator agreement (IAA) results underlying Figure~4a and the headline agreement statistic in the main text. For the cross-annotation study, three notes per dataset (4CE; CORAL) were independently re-annotated by a second annotator, blinded to the original assignment. We report agreement before (\emph{pre}) and after (\emph{post}) applying the five pre-specified structural-disambiguation guidelines (Figure~4b), using three complementary statistics: mention-level F1, Cohen's $\kappa$, and prevalence-and-bias-adjusted $\kappa$ (PABAK). F1-based agreement is our primary metric because the keep/discard class distribution is highly skewed ($\approx$75\% keep), conditions under which $\kappa$ is depressed by the base rate (``Cohen's paradox''); we therefore report all three for transparency.}
